# Analysis

## Layer 2, Head 5

This attention head captures the syntactic relationship between possessive determiners/pronouns and the nouns they modify. In the generated attention diagrams, words such as "his", "her", and "their" place strong attention weight directly on the noun immediately following them.

Example Sentences:
- The student forgot [MASK] notebook in the library.
- After the storm, [MASK] garden looked completely different.

## Layer 6, Head 3

This attention head models the connection between coordinating or subordinating conjunctions and the clauses they link together. Conjunctions such as "and", "but", "or", and "because" attend strongly to the words in the clause that follows them.

Example Sentences:
- We wanted to go hiking [MASK] the weather turned cold.
- He finished the report early [MASK] he could leave on time.
